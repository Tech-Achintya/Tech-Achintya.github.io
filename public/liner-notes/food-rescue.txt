The Food Rescue project is a web-based platform designed so as to decrease the amount of food wasted through the connection of hostel mess administrators with non-governmental organizations (NGOs). Mess administrators can post surplus food in real time, including its quantity and availability - while NGOs can browse and accept requests based on their location and the capacity of their organisation. The system streamlines coordination much more effectively by allowing for a very fast means of communication and scheduling pickups. Created using React for the frontend, Node. js with Express for the backend, and MySQL for database management, the platform really focuses on efficiency, scalability, and tangible, real-world results. Its objective is to make sure that excess food actually gets to people who really need it instead of simply discarding it.
